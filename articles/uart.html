Understanding UART Communication

UART (Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter) is the most common protocol for serial debugging.

How it Works:

Unlike SPI or I2C, UART is asynchronous, meaning it does not use a clock signal. Instead, both devices must agree on a Baud Rate (e.g., 9600 or 115200).

  • Start Bit: Signals the beginning of data.
  • Data Frame: Usually 8 bits of information.
  • Stop Bit: Signals the end of the packet.
